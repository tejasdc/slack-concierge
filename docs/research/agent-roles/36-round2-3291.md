2) One blocker in the core. Flow (c) sends his reply to the owning session and does not wake the Inbox, but every notification identity today is keyed to the conversation, meaning the Inbox session, plus the thread: the single slot, quiet repeats, withdrawal once read anywhere, presence suppression, and the iPhone's press-and-hold reply, which only replies to a conversation a notification named. Once answers come from other sessions, that key no longer matches who answers.

Smallest fix: key the slot on the thread, which is already the unit, and let the reply route resolve thread → current owning request → session. Then ownership can change without the slot moving, and the phone keeps working. Without it, a tap replies into the Inbox while the answer lives elsewhere.

Also small: 'what changed since he last read it' must come from the owner's reading mark, not be counted in the view. Counting in the view is how a row and its tab disagreed 3 against 4.
